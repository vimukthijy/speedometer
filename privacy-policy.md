## 1. Information the App accesses

When you choose **Start Tracking**, the App requests access to your device's precise location. It receives GPS location readings, including latitude, longitude, reported location accuracy, and the time of each reading.

The App uses these readings to calculate and display your current speed, maximum speed, average speed, and GPS accuracy. Speed and session statistics are derived from the location readings.

The App is designed for user-initiated tracking. It does not request the Android background-location permission. Location access is used while a tracking session is active and subject to Android and device location settings.

## 2. How information is used and stored

Location readings and the speed/session statistics calculated from them are processed locally on your device to provide the App's speed-tracking features.

The App does not send your precise location, speed, or session statistics to a server operated by the developer. The App does not create a user account and does not maintain a cloud-based tracking history.

During a session, the App temporarily holds the readings and calculated statistics in device memory. The App does not intentionally save a location history to persistent storage. Session information is cleared when you reset the session or when the App process is closed. Statistics may remain visible in the current app session after you stop tracking until you reset the session or close the App.

## 3. Sharing and third parties

The developer does not sell, rent, or share your location or speed information with advertisers or other third parties.

The App uses the location services provided by Android and your device to obtain GPS readings. Android, your device manufacturer, or the location-service provider may process information under their own terms and privacy practices. The App does not control those independent practices.

The App does not include advertising or analytics services in the version covered by this policy. This statement must be revised before release if advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs are added.

## 4. Permissions and your choices

The App requests Android location permission so it can read GPS information after you start a tracking session. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time through your Android device settings. If permission is denied, GPS speed tracking will not work.

You can stop a tracking session using the Stop Tracking control. You can clear the displayed session statistics using Reset Session.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

The App does not maintain a developer-operated database of your location or speed information. Readings are handled temporarily in device memory while the App is running. Reset Session clears the current session statistics held by the App. Closing or force-stopping the App clears its in-memory session data.

To stop future location access, revoke the App's location permission in Android settings or uninstall the App.

## 6. Security

Because the App processes tracking readings locally and does not transmit them to a developer-operated server, the developer does not maintain a remote copy of those readings. You should protect access to your device using the security controls provided by your device operating system.

## 7. Children's privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children. The App does not ask users to create accounts or submit personal details to the developer. If you believe the App is being used in a way that raises a privacy concern, contact the developer using the details below.
